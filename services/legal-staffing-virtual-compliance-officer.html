Overview

What our legal staffing and compliance retainers cover

Growing companies and law firms often have more legal operations work than a single in-house counsel can absorb: first-draft contracts from templates, redlines against a playbook, NDAs, vendor and customer agreements, tracking renewals and obligations, maintaining a contract repository, and research memos. A dedicated remote paralegal takes this volume on, works in your email, document and CLM tools, and follows your approval matrix. Work that requires an advocate — legal opinions, representation before courts and tribunals — remains with enrolled advocates, either your own counsel or ones you choose.

Contract lifecycle management (CLM) is the process from request through drafting, negotiation, signature, storage, obligation tracking and renewal. We set up or tidy up the process: intake forms, clause and template libraries, a searchable repository with key dates, and a monthly report of what is expiring or overdue. If you already use a CLM platform, we work in it; if not, we can run the process in tools you already have.

A virtual compliance officer is a fractional compliance function on a monthly retainer. It covers the company law calendar (board meetings, registers, MCA filings), coordination with auditors and tax advisers, statutory and labour-law registrations and returns, POSH policy and Internal Committee administration, and a monthly compliance status report for founders and the board. For companies that are legally required to appoint a whole-time Company Secretary, this supports but does not replace that appointment.

Frequently asked questions

Can a paralegal give legal advice or appear in court?

No. In India, practising law — giving legal opinions as an advocate and appearing before courts and tribunals — is reserved for advocates enrolled with a Bar Council. Our paralegals prepare drafts, research and documentation under the supervision of your counsel or our professionals; advice and representation remain with advocates.

How is a virtual compliance officer different from an in-house company secretary?

A virtual compliance officer is a fractional service on a monthly retainer, suited to companies that need the work done but not a full-time employee. Companies with paid-up share capital of ₹10 crore or more, and listed companies, must appoint a whole-time Company Secretary; for them, our service supports the CS rather than replacing them. See our comparison of the two models for details.

How do you protect confidentiality?

We sign a confidentiality and data-processing agreement before work starts, use role-based access to your systems rather than copying documents, keep an access log and remove access at the end of the engagement. Staff are bound by confidentiality obligations. If your sector has specific data-localisation or security requirements, we work within them.

What is contract lifecycle management?

CLM is the end-to-end handling of a contract: request, drafting, negotiation, approval, signature, storage, obligation tracking and renewal or termination. Good CLM means you can find any signed contract quickly, know when it renews and see who owes what under it.

How quickly can a paralegal start?

Usually within one to two weeks of signing the engagement, depending on the experience and language skills you need. We start with a short audit of your templates and repository so the paralegal is productive from the first month.

Do you handle POSH compliance?

We help draft the POSH policy, constitute the Internal Committee, maintain records and prepare the annual report your committee must submit. The Internal Committee must include an external member from an NGO or a person familiar with issues relating to sexual harassment; we can help you identify a suitable external member.

Can we scale the arrangement up or down?

Yes. Plans are reviewed quarterly and can move between part-time, full-time and project-based scopes. Notice periods are set out in the engagement letter so both sides can plan.
