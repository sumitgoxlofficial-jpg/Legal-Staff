Overview

What Private Limited registration involves

A private limited company is incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 through the integrated SPICe+ web form (INC-32) on the MCA V3 portal. Part A reserves the proposed name; Part B carries the incorporation details and is filed together with the linked forms: e-MoA (INC-33), e-AoA (INC-34) and AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35), which registers the company for PAN, TAN, EPFO and ESIC, opens the bank account and can apply for GSTIN at the same time. The INC-9 declaration of subscribers and directors is generated from the data in Part B.

You need at least two directors and two shareholders (the same people can be both), and at least one director must be resident in India under section 149(3). Every director needs a Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC); up to three new directors can be allotted a DIN directly through SPICe+. Part B must be certified by a practising CA, CS or CMA, and carries the declaration of the professional engaged in the formation.

Incorporation is not the finish line. Within 180 days the company must file INC-20A, the declaration of commencement of business with proof that the subscription money was received; until then it cannot commence business or borrow, and late filing attracts a penalty under section 10A. We plan the post-incorporation steps — first auditor, share certificates, first board meeting and registers — while the incorporation is still in progress.

Frequently asked questions

How long does Private Limited company registration take?

Usually 7 to 15 working days from receiving complete documents: one to two days for DSCs, two to four days for name approval and about a week for SPICe+ Part B processing. Resubmission remarks from the ROC or Central Registration Centre add time, which is why we check names and documents carefully before filing.

What is the minimum capital for a Private Limited company?

There is no minimum paid-up capital requirement under the Companies Act, 2013 since the 2015 amendment. Founders commonly start with an authorised capital of ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakh. Authorised capital affects the MCA fee and state stamp duty, so we recommend a figure that matches your near-term funding plan rather than an inflated one.

Can a foreign national or NRI be a director or shareholder?

Yes. A foreign national can be a director and shareholder, but at least one director must be resident in India. Foreign shareholding must comply with FEMA and the FDI policy for your sector, and the company reports the inflow of share capital to the RBI through the FIRMS portal after allotment. Foreign directors' identity and address documents must be notarised or apostilled as required.

Can I use my home address as the registered office?

Yes, a residential address can be the registered office if you have a recent utility bill and, where you are not the owner, a no-objection certificate from the owner. Check that your housing society or lease does not prohibit it, and remember that ROC notices and verification will come to this address.

What happens if INC-20A is not filed within 180 days?

Under section 10A the company cannot commence business or exercise borrowing powers until INC-20A is filed. Late filing attracts a penalty of ₹50,000 on the company and ₹1,000 per day on each officer in default, subject to a cap, and the ROC may initiate removal of the company's name if it is not carrying on business.

Is GST registration automatic with SPICe+?

No. AGILE-PRO-S lets you apply for GSTIN with the incorporation, but it is optional. GST is mandatory only once you cross the turnover threshold or fall in a compulsory category such as inter-state supply of goods or selling through e-commerce operators. We help you decide based on your business model.

Which is better for a startup: Private Limited or LLP?

If you plan to raise equity funding, issue ESOPs or bring in institutional investors, a Private Limited company is the usual choice. An LLP has lighter compliance and no tax on profit distribution, which suits professional and family businesses. Our Pvt Ltd vs LLP vs OPC comparison sets out the differences row by row.

Are the government fees included in your price?

No. Our fees are professional fees. MCA fees, stamp duty and other statutory charges are paid at actuals and we share every challan with you. The total government cost depends on your state and authorised capital.
