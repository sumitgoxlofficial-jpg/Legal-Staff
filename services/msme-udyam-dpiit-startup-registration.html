Overview

Recognition and registrations, and what each one gives you

DPIIT recognition under the Startup India scheme is available to a Private Limited company, LLP or registered partnership firm that is within ten years of incorporation, has not crossed the notified turnover limit (₹100 crore in any year under the current definition) and is working on innovation, development or improvement of products, processes or services, or on a scalable business model with high potential for employment or wealth creation. Recognition brings self-certification under certain labour and environment laws, relaxed public procurement norms, faster patent examination with fee rebates, and is the gateway to the tax benefits.

The section 80-IAC deduction lets an eligible startup deduct 100% of its profits for any three consecutive years out of its first ten. It is not automatic: a recognised company or LLP incorporated within the eligible window must apply to the Inter-Ministerial Board, which looks for genuine innovation and evidence of it. Most rejections come from vague descriptions, so we write the application around your product, technology and traction. (The Income-tax Act, 2025, effective 1 April 2026, carries the startup deduction forward under new section numbering; we follow the current provision when we file.)

Udyam registration is the free, Aadhaar-based self-declaration that classifies you as a micro, small or medium enterprise by investment and turnover. It unlocks MSME benefits such as protection against delayed payments under the MSMED Act, and buyers' tax deduction for payments to micro and small suppliers depends on paying within 45 days. GST registration is mandatory once turnover crosses the threshold or for compulsory categories such as inter-state supply of goods and e-commerce sellers.

Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible for DPIIT startup recognition?

A Private Limited company, LLP or registered partnership firm that is not more than ten years old from incorporation, whose turnover has not exceeded the notified limit (₹100 crore in any financial year under the current definition), and which is working on innovation or a scalable business model. It must not be formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business. DPIIT revises the definition from time to time, so we confirm the current criteria before applying.

Does DPIIT recognition automatically give the 80-IAC tax exemption?

No. Recognition is a prerequisite, but the tax holiday requires a separate application to the Inter-Ministerial Board. Only companies and LLPs incorporated within the eligible window qualify, and the Board certifies eligibility after assessing innovation. Registered partnership firms are not eligible for 80-IAC.

Why do 80-IAC applications get rejected?

The common reasons are a business description that reads like a regular trading or service business, no evidence of the product working, missing financial statements, or claims of innovation that are not explained. A focused write-up with demonstrable evidence makes a significant difference.

Is Udyam registration free?

Yes. Udyam registration on the government portal has no fee and is based on Aadhaar and PAN self-declaration. Be wary of look-alike websites that charge for it while appearing official. Our fee is for preparing and filing correctly and linking it to your other registrations.

What are the MSME limits for micro, small and medium enterprises?

Classification is based on both investment in plant and machinery or equipment and annual turnover. The limits were revised upward with effect from 1 April 2025 — micro up to ₹2.5 crore investment and ₹10 crore turnover, small up to ₹25 crore and ₹100 crore, medium up to ₹125 crore and ₹500 crore. Check the current notification as these are reviewed periodically.

When is GST registration mandatory?

When aggregate turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for suppliers of goods (in most states) or ₹20 lakh for service providers, with lower limits in some special category states. It is mandatory regardless of turnover for inter-state supplies of goods, sellers on e-commerce platforms and certain other categories. Voluntary registration is allowed and often needed to work with larger customers.

Can an LLP get DPIIT recognition?

Yes. LLPs are eligible for DPIIT recognition and, if incorporated within the eligible window, can apply for the 80-IAC deduction. Some benefits aimed at companies, such as those linked to share issues, do not apply to LLPs.
