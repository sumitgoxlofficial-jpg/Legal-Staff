How it works

From first step to daily habit

  1. Add your cases

    Create cases with court and case number, or import case details by CNR where a court-data provider is connected.

  2. Confirm dates from orders

    The order reader proposes each next date; confirmed dates land in the diary with their purpose.

  3. Check the week

    Use the week view to plan appearances across courts and spot clashes before the day.

  4. Reassign when needed

    Move a hearing to a junior; the diary, the hearing board and the reminders update together.

  5. Start the day with the briefing

    Read the morning briefing, or export the diary as .ics to see it alongside your other calendars.

Frequently asked questions

Does the diary pull cause lists automatically?

No. Legal Staff does not scrape cause lists. Dates come from orders you confirm or entries you make. Keep checking your court’s cause list on the day, since listings can change.

What counts as a clash?

Two hearings at the same time for the same advocate. The diary flags them so you can reassign one or plan around it.

Can I see the diary in Google Calendar or Outlook?

You can export the diary as an .ics file, which calendar apps such as Google Calendar and Outlook can import.

Is there a mobile app?

Legal Staff is a web app. There are no native iOS or Android apps, but it works in mobile browsers on your phone.

Can my juniors see my diary?

Team members see hearings according to their roles and the matters they work on, so a junior can see what is assigned to them.

What is in the morning briefing?

The day’s hearings with court and purpose where recorded, along with what is due, so the day starts with a single list.

Can I move my existing diary over?

Yes. Add your running matters and their next dates; after that, each new order keeps the diary up to date once you confirm the date.
