How it works

From first step to daily habit

  1. Invite your team

    Add juniors, associates and clerks with roles that fit what they do.

  2. Assign tasks to matters

    Create tasks such as filing, inspection or drafting with a due date and an owner.

  3. Run the day from the board

    The hearing board shows today’s matters, who is appearing and which tasks are still open.

  4. Keep clients informed

    Store client details and share selected updates and documents through the client portal.

  5. Invoice per matter

    Raise invoices with GST against each matter and review reports on the chamber’s workload.

Who it’s for

Senior advocates with a team of juniors

Frequently asked questions

Can I control what each team member sees?

Yes. Members join with roles, and roles decide what they can see and do in the firm’s workspace.

What is the hearing board?

A daily view of the matters listed today, with court, item, the person appearing and the tasks still open for each matter.

Can juniors use it on their phones?

Yes, in a mobile browser. Legal Staff is a web app and has no native iOS or Android apps.

Does it handle GST invoices?

Yes. You can raise per-matter invoices with GST. Check the GST treatment that applies to your practice with your tax adviser.

Can clients log in?

You can share selected updates and documents with clients through the client portal. They see only what you share about their matters.

Which plan do I need for a team?

Legal Staff has Solo, Professional, Firm and Enterprise plans. See the pricing section for what each plan includes, or book a demo to talk it through.
