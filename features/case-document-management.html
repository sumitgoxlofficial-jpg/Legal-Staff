How it works

From first step to daily habit

  1. Open the case

    Every case has its own document vault from the moment it is created.

  2. Upload what you have

    Add petitions, replies, annexures, orders, vakalatnamas and certified copies to the right folder.

  3. Mark the evidence

    Record exhibit marks against evidence as the matter proceeds.

  4. Draft in place

    Start from a Drafting Studio template, keep versions and export to DOCX or PDF.

  5. Share selectively

    Share chosen documents with the client through the client portal; everything else stays inside the firm.

Frequently asked questions

What can I store in a case vault?

Pleadings, orders, annexures, vakalatnamas, certified copies and evidence, in common formats such as PDF, Word documents and images.

Who can see the documents?

Only members of your firm, according to their roles. Each firm’s data is isolated with row-level security, and storage is private.

Can I share documents with a client?

Yes. You can share selected documents through the client portal. Nothing is shared unless you choose it.

Does it replace certified copies?

No. A scan in the vault is for reference and preparation; courts and authorities still need certified copies where the rules require them.

Can I draft documents here?

Yes. The Drafting Studio has templates such as legal notice, section 138 notice, bail application, petition, written statement, reply, appeal, revision, affidavit and agreement, with versions and DOCX/PDF export.

Can the AI read the documents in the vault?

Yes. The AI Case Brain answers questions from the case’s documents with citations, for an advocate to review.
