Chamber talk scattered across personal apps

  • Work mixed with personal chatsInstructions about a matter sit between family messages on a personal phone, and are hard to find a week later.
  • Files sent to the wrong groupA draft or an order forwarded in a hurry can land in a group that includes people outside the chamber.
  • People who leave keep the historyWhen a junior or clerk moves on, the chamber’s conversations stay on their phone.

With Legal Staff

One private chat for the chamber

  • Files, meetings and reactionsSend PDFs and other files, post a Google Meet link as a card that opens the meeting, and react to a message with emoji.

How it works

From first step to daily habit

  1. Invite your team

    Add members with their work email. They receive an invitation and join the firm the first time they sign in.

  2. Open Team chat

    Team chat is in the sidebar under Firm. The team conversation is there for every active member.

  3. Message everyone or one person

    Write in the team chat, or pick a colleague to start a one-to-one chat. New messages appear live.

  4. Share files and meeting links

    Attach a PDF or another file, or paste a Google Meet link to post it as a meeting card.

  5. React and move on

    Acknowledge a message with an emoji reaction instead of another reply.

Who it’s for

Built for the way litigators work

Senior advocates with juniors in different courts

Send one instruction to everyone in the morning and follow up with the junior concerned in a one-to-one chat.

Chambers that want work kept off personal phones

Chamber conversations and files stay in the firm’s workspace, with the people who are members today.

Firms with partners and associates

Call a quick meeting by posting a Google Meet link where the whole team will see it.

Frequently asked questions

Who can read the team chat?

Every active member of your firm. People outside the firm cannot see it.

Are one-to-one chats private?

Yes. A one-to-one chat can be read only by the two members in it.

Can I send files?

Yes. You can attach PDFs and other files to a message. There is a size limit for each file, shown in the app when you attach one.

Does it make video calls?

No. You paste a Google Meet link and Legal Staff shows it as a meeting card that opens the meeting in Google Meet.

Is it the same as WhatsApp reminders?

No. Team chat is for conversations inside your firm. WhatsApp reminders are hearing reminders sent to your phone through the WhatsApp Business API once connected.

Can clients join the chat?

No. Team chat is for members of the firm. Clients see only what you share with them through the client portal.

Is there a mobile app?

Legal Staff is a web app. Team chat works in a mobile browser; there are no native iOS or Android apps.
