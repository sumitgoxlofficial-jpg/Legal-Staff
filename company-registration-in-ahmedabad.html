Overview

Why founders incorporate in Ahmedabad

Ahmedabad is Gujarat’s commercial capital, with strong textile, pharmaceutical, chemicals and engineering industries and a fast-growing startup scene. Proximity to GIFT City in Gandhinagar also attracts fintech and financial-services founders, and an Ahmedabad registered office places the company under RoC Ahmedabad.

This page covers Ahmedabad specifically. For rules that apply across the state — and other cities in it — see company registration in Gujarat.

Jurisdiction

Which RoC covers Ahmedabad

Registrar for a registered office in Ahmedabad

RoC Ahmedabad

Jurisdiction: State of Gujarat (and Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu).

SPICe+ applications are approved by the Central Registration Centre; after that, the company’s annual and event-based filings on the MCA V3 portal are administered by RoC Ahmedabad. Other places in our guides under the same Registrar: Surat, Gujarat.

Office addresses are on the MCA website’s RoC contact page; filings are online, so a visit is rarely needed.

What this means in Ahmedabad

Gujarat stamp duty on the MoA and AoA is paid through SPICe+. Leave-and-licence and lease agreements for Ahmedabad premises attract Gujarat stamp duty, typically on e-stamp paper.

Registered office

Registered-office tips for Ahmedabad

  • A unit inside GIFT IFSC has additional IFSCA requirements; incorporate with a GIFT City address only once the premises and the IFSCA licensing route are confirmed.
  • GIDC estate plots are leasehold; check that the allotment letter permits your activity before using the unit as a registered office.
  • For a home office in Ahmedabad, the owner’s NOC and a recent Torrent Power or other electricity bill are normally sufficient.

Address proof the RoC expects

  • Utility bill not older than two months
  • Owner’s or operator’s NOC
  • Rent or leave-and-licence agreement

Company registration in Ahmedabad: FAQs

Which RoC handles companies registered in Ahmedabad?

Companies with a registered office in Gujarat, including Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar, are under the Registrar of Companies, Ahmedabad. The same office also covers the Union Territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.

Can I register a fintech company in GIFT City?

Yes, but an IFSC unit is a regulated entity: in addition to company incorporation with a GIFT City address, it needs registration or a licence from the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) for its activity. Plan the IFSCA application alongside incorporation.

Does Gujarat charge professional tax?

Yes. Gujarat levies professional tax, administered through municipal corporations in cities such as Ahmedabad, so both company enrolment and employer registration are needed once you hire.

Is a private limited company or an LLP better for a Gujarat manufacturing business?

A private limited company is usually preferred when you need bank finance, equity investors or government subsidies that require a company. An LLP suits closely held businesses that want lighter compliance. Our Pvt Ltd vs LLP vs OPC comparison sets out the trade-offs.
