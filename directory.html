Questions clients ask

Is it free for me?+

Yes. Searching, comparing and sending a contact request cost nothing and need no account. You agree fees directly with the advocate.

Do you tell me who the “best” advocate is?+

No. Bar Council rules do not allow advocates to be ranked or advertised, and the right advocate depends on your matter. We show verified facts and let you compare, and results are always listed alphabetically.

How do I know an advocate is real?+

Look for the blue tick. It means the advocate uploaded their enrolment certificate and photo ID and our system read them and matched the name, enrolment number, State Bar Council and year to the profile. It is not a check against the Bar Council roll, so you can still ask to see the certificate and confirm the number with the State Bar Council. Profiles without the tick are marked “Not verified yet”.

What happens after I send a request?+

Only the advocate you chose sees your name, contact details and matter, and only with your consent. They decide whether to reply, and you can withdraw your request at any time.

Can Legal Staff handle my case or give legal advice?+

No. Legal Staff does not give legal advice, assign matters or take any share of fees. It only helps you find and contact advocates yourself.

Verify before you hire

How to verify an advocate is genuine.

  1. ✓Look for the blue tick on the photo. It means Legal Staff read the advocate's enrolment certificate and photo ID and matched the name, enrolment number, State Bar Council and year to the profile.
  2. 2Note the enrolment number and State Bar Council shown on the profile, and check them below.
  3. 3Cross-check with the Bar Council. The blue tick is not a Bar Council check. The State Bar Council keeps the roll of enrolled advocates and can confirm a number.
  4. 4Ask to see the documents at your first meeting: the enrolment certificate, Certificate of Practice and a photo ID.
Something looks wrong? Report a profile and a moderator will check it. Never pay a stranger in advance.

Questions advocates ask

Is ₹49 really the only payment?+

Yes. ₹49 is charged once when you create your profile. There is no renewal, no monthly fee and no charge per lead.

What does “lifetime” mean?+

Your profile stays listed for as long as your enrolment is valid and the Legal Staff Directory is in operation. If a profile is found to be inaccurate or the enrolment lapses, it can be suspended.

How do I get leads?+

Clients search by city, court and practice area, open your profile and send a contact request with their matter. You see every request in your dashboard and decide whether to reply. Legal Staff does not assign matters or take any part of your fees.

Does paying ₹49 rank me higher?+

No. Every advocate pays the same ₹49 and results are listed alphabetically. The fee covers your listing, not a better position.

What do I need to apply?+

A Google account, your enrolment number and State Bar Council, your enrolment certificate, a government photo ID and, if you like, a profile photo. It takes about five minutes.

What if my documents cannot be verified?+

Your profile stays listed, without the blue tick. We tell you exactly what did not match or was unclear, and you can upload clearer documents and we check again automatically. See our refund policy for the fee.

Is this the same as the Legal Staff practice software?+

No. The directory profile is separate from the practice management software, which has its own plans.
