How they work

Calculations you can check

Each tool runs in your browser: no sign-in, nothing you type is sent anywhere. Every result shows its working — the section, the excluded days and the arithmetic — so you can check it against the bare Act before you rely on it.

Inside Legal Staff, the same rules sit behind the limitation tracker: dates are calculated with every exclusion shown, wait for your approval, and then reach the court diary and WhatsApp reminders.

General information for advocates, not legal advice. Statutes, rules and court practice directions change; check the bare Act, the latest amendments and your court's rules before relying on any date or section.
