eCourts app vs Legal Staff

eCourts Services app vs Legal Staff: case status lookups and a chamber workspace

The eCourts Services app is the official, free way to check case status, listings and orders on the eCourts network, and it remains the source of record. Legal Staff is not a replacement for it. It is the workspace a chamber uses around it: reading orders, keeping a shared diary, sending reminders and holding the file.

When to choose which

Pick the option that fits your stage

Choose eCourts Services app

eCourts on its own is enough when…

  • You only need to check the status or next date of a few cases
  • You work alone and keep your own diary
  • You do not need reminders, documents or tasks in one place
Choose Legal Staff

Add Legal Staff when…

  • You run many matters across courts and need one diary for the chamber
  • You want orders summarised and dates confirmed, not copied
  • Juniors, clerks and clients need the right information without calls
  • You want the case file, research and billing in the same place

Use both: eCourts for the record, Legal Staff for the work

The eCourts Services app is free, official and the place to verify status, listings and orders. Nothing on this page suggests otherwise, and you should keep using it.

What eCourts is not built to be is a chamber’s workspace. Legal Staff sits on top: you paste or upload the order, it summarises it and proposes the next date, you confirm, and the diary, hearing board and reminders follow. Documents, tasks, client updates and invoices live in the same case.

Because eCourts does not offer an open public API, Legal Staff does not scrape it. Case import by CNR works where a court-data provider is connected, and you should always check the official record when in doubt.

General information for advocates, not legal advice. Statutes, rules and court practice directions change; check the bare Act, the latest amendments and your court's rules before relying on any date or section.

Frequently asked questions

Does Legal Staff replace the eCourts app?

No. eCourts is the official, free case-information service and remains the source of record. Legal Staff adds order reading, a shared diary, reminders, documents, team and research on top.

Does Legal Staff pull data from eCourts automatically?

eCourts has no open public API, and Legal Staff does not scrape it. Case details can be imported by CNR where a court-data provider is connected; otherwise you add the case and paste or upload orders.

If both show a next date, which one do I trust?

The court’s official record. Legal Staff shows the date you confirmed from the order; if the listing changes, check the cause list and update the diary.

Is the eCourts app free?

Yes. It is an official service provided free of charge. Legal Staff is paid software with Solo, Professional, Firm and Enterprise plans.

Does Legal Staff cover High Courts and tribunals too?

Yes. You can keep matters from the Supreme Court, High Courts, district courts and tribunals in one diary, with dates confirmed from their orders.

Is there a Legal Staff mobile app?

Legal Staff is a web app that works in mobile browsers. There are no native iOS or Android apps.
