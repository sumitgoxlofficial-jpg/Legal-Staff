When to choose which

Pick the option that fits your stage

Choose Typical enterprise legal ERP

An enterprise legal ERP may suit you when…

  • You run a corporate legal department with contracts, compliance and litigation in one system
  • You need custom workflows, approvals and management reporting
  • You have the time and budget for an implementation project and training
Choose Legal Staff

Legal Staff fits better when…

  • You are a litigator, chamber or litigation firm and court work is the core of your day
  • You want to be working the same day, without a configuration project
  • Order reading, the court diary and WhatsApp reminders matter more than legal-ops dashboards
  • You want a published plan rather than a custom quote

Different tools for different jobs

Enterprise legal ERPs are built to cover a lot of ground, and for a corporate legal department that breadth and configurability can be exactly what is needed. The trade-off is usually the effort to implement, configure and train, and a cost model built around larger organisations.

Legal Staff takes the opposite approach: it is narrower, aimed at the advocate in court. Orders are read and the next date is proposed for your confirmation, the diary flags clashes, reminders go out on WhatsApp and email, and documents, tasks and invoices sit with the case.

Before you decide, list what your team does every day, ask each vendor to show those exact tasks, and check their current features, pricing and terms directly with them.

Frequently asked questions

Is Legal Staff an alternative to Provakil, ManageMyLawsuits or PracticeLeague?

It can be, depending on your needs. Legal Staff is litigation-first software for advocates and chambers. Compare it with any product you are considering on the tasks your team does every day, and check each vendor’s current offering directly.

Does this page compare specific vendors?

No. It compares Legal Staff with a typical enterprise legal ERP in general terms. Features and pricing of named products change, so check them with each vendor.

How long does it take to set up Legal Staff?

Most chambers can sign in with Google, add their running matters and start using the diary the same day.

Can Legal Staff handle a large firm?

Legal Staff has Firm and Enterprise plans with team roles, task assignment, a hearing board and reports. If you also need contract management or compliance workflows, a broader legal ERP may suit you better.

Is my data separate from other firms?

Yes. Each firm’s data is isolated with row-level security, and documents are kept in private storage.

Does Legal Staff have a mobile app?

It is a web app that works in mobile browsers. There are no native iOS or Android apps.
