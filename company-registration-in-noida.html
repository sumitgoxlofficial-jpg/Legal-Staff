Overview

Why founders incorporate in Noida

Noida and Greater Noida are planned industrial townships in Uttar Pradesh where IT services firms, electronics manufacturers and edtech and media companies have set up large campuses. Founders choose Noida for lower office costs than Delhi or Gurugram while staying within NCR, and a Noida address places the company under the Uttar Pradesh Registrar at Kanpur.

This page covers Noida specifically. For rules that apply across the state — and other cities in it — see company registration in Uttar Pradesh.

Jurisdiction

Which RoC covers Noida

Registrar for a registered office in Noida

RoC Kanpur

Jurisdiction: State of Uttar Pradesh.

SPICe+ applications are approved by the Central Registration Centre; after that, the company’s annual and event-based filings on the MCA V3 portal are administered by RoC Kanpur. Other places in our guides under the same Registrar: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.

Office addresses are on the MCA website’s RoC contact page; filings are online, so a visit is rarely needed.

What this means in Noida

A Noida registered office pays Uttar Pradesh stamp duty on the MoA and AoA through SPICe+. Office leases in Noida are executed on UP e-stamp paper, and leases of authority-allotted property may also need the development authority’s permission.

Registered office

Registered-office tips for Noida

  • Much of Noida is leasehold land allotted by the development authority. If you rent an industrial or institutional plot, check that the lease deed permits your use and sub-letting before you rely on it as a registered office.
  • Residential addresses in Noida sectors are accepted by the RoC with an owner’s NOC, but local authority rules may restrict running a commercial office from them — use the address only for registered-office purposes if in doubt.
  • Noida and Ghaziabad are both under RoC Kanpur; moving to Delhi or Gurugram later is a change of state that needs Regional Director approval.

Address proof the RoC expects

  • Utility bill not older than two months
  • Owner’s or operator’s NOC
  • Rent or leave-and-licence agreement

Company registration in Noida: FAQs

Which RoC is a Noida company registered under?

Even though Noida is in NCR, companies with a registered office in Uttar Pradesh fall under the Registrar of Companies at Kanpur, not the Delhi Registrar. Since filings are online, this rarely affects day-to-day work, but it matters for any matter that needs the RoC’s attention.

Is Noida a good place to register an electronics or hardware startup?

Noida has an established electronics manufacturing ecosystem and authority-allotted industrial land, which is why many hardware businesses register here. Factory licences, pollution-board consents and land allotment run separately from company incorporation, so plan those in parallel.

Can I use my Noida flat as the registered office?

Yes, the Companies Act allows a residential address as a registered office. You need proof of address (a recent utility bill) and an NOC from the owner if the flat is not in a director’s name. Using the flat as a working office with staff may need permissions under local rules.

Does a Noida company also need GST registration in Delhi?

Only if it has a place of business in Delhi. A Noida company supplying Delhi customers from its Noida office registers for GST in Uttar Pradesh and charges IGST on interstate supplies. If you also keep a Delhi office or warehouse, you register in Delhi for that place too.
