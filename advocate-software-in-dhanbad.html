Practising in Dhanbad

What a Dhanbad litigation practice looks like

Dhanbad sits at the centre of India’s coal belt. Alongside a busy civil and criminal docket, industrial disputes involving coal-sector employers and workers are a long-standing part of local practice, and appeals and writs travel to the Jharkhand High Court in Ranchi.

Legal Staff gives a Dhanbad chamber one diary for district courts, industrial tribunals and the High Court. Orders are summarised and next dates confirmed, juniors get WhatsApp reminders, and each matter’s documents sit in its own private vault.

Questions from advocates in Dhanbad

Can Legal Staff track industrial tribunal matters?

Yes. Add the tribunal as the forum; orders, documents and reminders work as for any court.

Can a tribunal award and the writ against it be kept together?

Yes. Link the writ in the Jharkhand High Court to the tribunal matter under the same client, with the award, the orders and the next dates for both on file.

Can I raise invoices for corporate clients?

Yes. Per-matter invoices with GST can be raised from the case.
