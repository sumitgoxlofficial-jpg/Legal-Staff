Overview

Why founders incorporate in Bangalore

Bengaluru (still widely called Bangalore) is where many Indian software, SaaS and deep-tech founders choose to incorporate, because the hiring pool, investor networks and customer base for technology businesses are concentrated here. A Bengaluru registered office also places the company under RoC Bengaluru, which handles a very large volume of startup filings, so routine post-incorporation changes are well-trodden ground.

This page covers Bangalore specifically. For rules that apply across the state — and other cities in it — see company registration in Karnataka.

Jurisdiction

Which RoC covers Bangalore

Registrar for a registered office in Bangalore

RoC Bengaluru

Jurisdiction: State of Karnataka.

SPICe+ applications are approved by the Central Registration Centre; after that, the company’s annual and event-based filings on the MCA V3 portal are administered by RoC Bengaluru. Other places in our guides under the same Registrar: Karnataka.

Office addresses are on the MCA website’s RoC contact page; filings are online, so a visit is rarely needed.

What this means in Bangalore

Karnataka stamp duty on the MoA and AoA is paid electronically with the SPICe+ filing. Office lease or leave-and-licence agreements for a Bengaluru office are a separate stamp-duty event and are usually executed on Karnataka e-stamp paper.

Registered office

Registered-office tips for Bangalore

  • Co-working spaces in Koramangala, HSR Layout and Indiranagar routinely issue a registered-office NOC with a utility bill; ask for both before you file, since SPICe+ needs the bill in the owner’s or operator’s name.
  • If you use a founder’s rented flat, get the landlord’s NOC in writing — many Bengaluru rental agreements prohibit commercial use, and a later dispute can force an INC-22 change of address.
  • Karnataka levies professional tax on salaries, so register the company under the Karnataka professional tax law as soon as you hire (AGILE-PRO-S can be used to apply at incorporation).

Address proof the RoC expects

  • Utility bill not older than two months
  • Owner’s or operator’s NOC
  • Rent or leave-and-licence agreement

Company registration in Bangalore: FAQs

Which RoC registers a company with a Bangalore address?

Incorporation applications filed on SPICe+ are processed centrally by the Central Registration Centre, but once incorporated a company with its registered office anywhere in Karnataka — including Bengaluru — falls under the Registrar of Companies, Bengaluru. Later filings such as INC-22, MGT-7 and AOC-4 are tracked against that RoC.

Can I register my Bangalore startup at a co-working space?

Yes. The Companies Act only requires a registered office capable of receiving communications. A co-working operator’s NOC, a recent utility bill for the premises and your agreement with the operator are normally accepted. Keep the agreement current, because the RoC can physically verify the office.

Do I need a Shops and Establishments registration in Bengaluru?

Commercial establishments in Karnataka are covered by the Karnataka Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1961. Once you operate from an office with staff, registration under that Act is generally required; we confirm the current process with the labour department portal when we set up your compliances.

Is a Karnataka company eligible for state startup grants?

Karnataka’s startup policy offers recognition and grant schemes such as Elevate to eligible startups registered in the state. Eligibility rules and calls for applications change each round, so check the Karnataka Startup Cell’s current notification before applying; DPIIT recognition under Startup India is a separate, national scheme.
