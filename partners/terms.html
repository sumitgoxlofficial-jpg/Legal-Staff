Partner Terms

Status of this document

This is the working draft of the Partner Terms. It must be reviewed by qualified Indian legal counsel before any reward is activated for any category. Until a category’s arrangement has been approved and recorded, no reward is generated for it, whatever any screen or message says.

What a partner is

A partner is an independent person or business who tells practising advocates about the LegalStaff Directory. A partner is not an employee, agent or representative of LegalStaff, cannot bind LegalStaff, and must not describe themselves as one.

Conduct

  • Do not promise or imply that a listing will bring clients, cases, income or professional success.
  • Do not solicit on behalf of any advocate, offer inducements to advocates, or share any part of an advocate’s fees. Advocates remain bound by the rules of their Bar Council and the Bar Council of India, including Rule 36 of the BCI Rules on advertising and solicitation.
  • Do not misstate LegalStaff’s verification: only LegalStaff decides whether a listing is approved.
  • Use only the approved marketing material, or material that does not contradict it.
  • Do not create fake registrations, refer yourself, or manipulate links, QR codes or payments.
  • Comply with all laws that apply to you, including consent rules for messaging and data protection law.

Referrals and attribution

A referral is recorded by LegalStaff’s servers when an advocate follows your link or QR code and then submits a directory application. The first valid referral within the attribution window (30 days by default) is kept. A referral cannot be moved to another partner after it is recorded. Directory applications made without a referral, or made before a link was followed, are not attributed. Self-referrals and referrals that match your own details are rejected.

Rewards

Where the programme terms for your category have been approved and switched on, a fixed reward may be recorded when every condition is met: the referral is valid, the advocate is genuine and unique, a payment has been verified (if the listing requires one), required documents are submitted, LegalStaff has approved the listing, and no fraud flag is outstanding. Rewards are reversed if the listing is rejected, refunded or found to be fraudulent. LegalStaff may change reward amounts, and may switch a category on or off, for future referrals.

A reward is not guaranteed and is not payment for legal work.

Payouts

Approved rewards are paid by UPI or bank transfer once you have completed KYC and your approved balance reaches the minimum withdrawal. Payouts may be paused, held or refused where there is a compliance or fraud concern. You are responsible for any tax on what you receive.

Suspension and termination

LegalStaff may suspend or terminate a partner account for breach of these terms, suspected fraud or any conduct that harms advocates or LegalStaff. Existing records are kept for audit and legal reasons.

Changes

We may update these terms. The version you accepted is recorded with your application, and you will be asked to accept a new version where it matters.

This policy is provided for information and should be reviewed by your legal adviser.
