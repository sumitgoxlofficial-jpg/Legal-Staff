How this is calculated

What goes into the cost of incorporating

Every line in the estimate maps to a statutory fee or a market cost. Here is the logic, and where it is approximate.

MCA fees

Name reservation through SPICe+ Part A or RUN costs ₹1,000. For companies, the SPICe+ incorporation fee is nil where authorised capital is up to ₹15 lakh; above that, the registration-fee schedule in the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014 applies (₹5,000 up to ₹1 lakh, then a per-₹10,000 rate that falls as capital rises). LLP incorporation fees in FiLLiP run by contribution slab, from ₹500 up to ₹1 lakh to ₹5,000 above ₹10 lakh.

Stamp duty

Stamp duty on the MoA and AoA is a state levy, so it depends on where the registered office is. Some states charge a flat amount, others a percentage of authorised capital with a cap. Since SPICe+, the duty is paid through the MCA portal for most states. LLP agreements are stamped separately on the state e-stamp system, usually linked to contribution.

PAN, TAN and DSC

PAN and TAN are applied for within the incorporation form; the small processing fees are shown as indicative. Each director or designated partner needs a Class 3 digital signature certificate; prices vary by vendor and validity, so the default of ₹1,500 per person is editable.

Assumptions

  • Company estimates assume a company with share capital, standard SPICe+ filing with e-MoA (INC-33) and e-AoA (INC-34), and DINs allotted in SPICe+ (available for up to three proposed directors).
  • The registration fee above ₹15 lakh applies the general schedule; the MCA fee calculator may apply different rates for specific classes of company, so treat that line as indicative.
  • Not included: stamp duty on share certificates, notarisation or apostille of foreign documents, office-address documents, GST registration, bank charges and any resubmission costs.
  • Professional fees, when switched on, show the starting price of our plans; government fees are always charged at actuals.

Frequently asked questions

Is company registration free for capital up to ₹15 lakh?

The MCA incorporation fee in SPICe+ is nil for companies with authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh. You still pay name reservation, state stamp duty on the MoA and AoA, PAN and TAN processing, and the cost of digital signatures, so the total is never zero.

Why does stamp duty differ between states?

Stamp duty is levied under each state’s stamp law (or the Indian Stamp Act as adopted by that state). Some states charge a flat amount on the MoA and AoA; others charge a percentage of authorised capital with a cap. The registered office decides which state’s duty applies.

Does a higher authorised capital cost more?

Yes, above ₹15 lakh the MCA registration fee rises with authorised capital, and in states that charge AoA stamp duty as a percentage, stamp duty rises too. Many founders start with a modest authorised capital and increase it later through SH-7, which has its own fee and stamp duty.

How is LLP registration cost different?

LLPs pay a FiLLiP fee by contribution slab, a Form 3 fee for filing the LLP agreement, and stamp duty on the LLP agreement under the state stamp law. There is no MoA or AoA. Form 3 must be filed within 30 days of incorporation, after which ₹100 per day of delay applies.

Are these figures final?

No. They are indicative estimates based on published schedules as we understand them. Government fees and stamp duty are revised from time to time, and the MCA portal computes the payable amount at filing. Confirm before you pay, or ask us to prepare the filing.
