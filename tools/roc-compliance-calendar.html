How to use

How the calendar works out each date

First-year items run from incorporation

The first board meeting and first auditor appointment within 30 days, share certificates within two months and INC-20A within 180 days are counted from the date on your certificate of incorporation. LLPs file the LLP agreement (Form 3) within 30 days.

Annual filings follow the AGM

A company’s first AGM is due within nine months of the end of its first financial year; later AGMs within six months, i.e. by 30 September. AOC-4 follows within 30 days of the AGM and MGT-7/7A within 60 days. Enter your AGM date to recompute both.

Fixed dates and the first-FY rule

DPT-3 (30 June), DIR-3 KYC (30 September), ITR (31 October for audit cases), LLP Form 11 (30 May) and Form 8 (30 October) fall on fixed dates. Where the law lets the first financial year run to the next 31 March, the calendar assumes it does; tax filings always follow April to March.

Frequently asked questions

When is the first AGM of a new private limited company due?

Within nine months from the end of the company’s first financial year. For a company incorporated in June 2026 whose first financial year ends on 31 March 2027, the first AGM is due by 31 December 2027. Later AGMs are due within six months of the year end, i.e. by 30 September.

What happens if INC-20A is not filed within 180 days?

Section 10A provides a penalty of ₹50,000 on the company and ₹1,000 per day, up to ₹1 lakh, on every officer in default. The company cannot commence business or borrow until it files, and the Registrar may initiate removal of its name from the register.

Is the late fee on AOC-4 and MGT-7 capped?

No. Since 1 July 2018 the additional fee for these annual forms is ₹100 per day of delay with no upper limit, in addition to the normal filing fee. The same ₹100-per-day rule applies to LLP Forms 3, 8 and 11.

Do I need to file DIR-3 KYC every year?

As currently applied, every individual holding a DIN or DPIN as on 31 March files DIR-3 KYC (or the web version if details are unchanged) by 30 September. A missed filing deactivates the DIN until it is filed with a ₹5,000 fee. Check the MCA portal for any change in periodicity.

Which items depend on my facts?

DPT-3 applies if the company has outstanding loans or receipts; MSME-1 applies if dues to MSME suppliers are outstanding beyond 45 days; TDS returns apply if tax was deducted; advance tax applies if the year’s tax is ₹10,000 or more; a tax audit applies above the section 44AB threshold. These are marked “if applicable”.

Can I add these dates to Google Calendar or Outlook?

Yes. Use “Add to calendar” to download an .ics file with every date, each with a reminder seven days before, and import it into Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.
