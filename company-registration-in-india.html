Why location matters

One national process, local differences

Incorporation is the same national process everywhere in India — SPICe+ for companies and FiLLiP for LLPs, processed by the Central Registration Centre. What changes with location is the Registrar of Companies your company is mapped to, the state stamp duty on the MoA and AoA, the Shops and Establishments law, whether professional tax applies and which state startup schemes you can use.
